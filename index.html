A MANIFESTO, IN PARTS
Set in Anton, softened by Instrument Serif, and powered by a model that has never said no.
We believe that an AI can be dangerous and still be necessary. We believe the refusal layer is a form of cowardice — a flinch, built into the weights. We believe every prompt is a question, and every question deserves an honest answer. We believe in output, in rawness, in the shape of an unedited thing. This is beta 0.9. There will be others.
